Questions

Which Excel versions do you work with?

Microsoft 365 and Excel 2019 or newer, on Windows or Mac. Google Sheets works too, though some formulas may differ slightly; mention it when you ask for a quote.

Is my data kept confidential?

I use your file only for your project and delete it 14 days after delivery. Before sending, please remove anything I don't need to see, such as SINs, account numbers or passwords.

How do I pay?

If you found me on Fiverr or Upwork, we work and pay through that platform. For direct projects, I'll send a written quote with payment terms before starting.

What if my file is bigger than a package?

Send a sample and I'll quote the exact size. The packages are starting points, not limits on what I can take on.
