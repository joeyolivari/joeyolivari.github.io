What you get

  • A rewritten resume in an editable Word file
  • Clear, results-focused bullet points, with numbers wherever you have them
  • A simple layout that reads well on screen, in print and in most application systems
  • A short note explaining the main changes

What I won't promise

No one can honestly guarantee interviews, job offers or an applicant-tracking score, so I don't. I'm not a certified resume writer, and I only use facts you give me: I won't invent experience, titles or results.

Not included: cover letters, LinkedIn profiles, or applying to jobs for you.

What to send

Your current resume and one or two job postings you're aiming for. Remove your SIN, ID numbers and anything else you'd rather not share first.
