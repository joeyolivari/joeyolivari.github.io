PRIVACY NOTE

How your details are handled

Short version: the site has no client database. Contact inquiries are delivered through a third-party form service, and I use your details to respond to your request.

This website

The site is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no tracking scripts, analytics, or cookies of its own, and no database. When you submit the inquiry form, your entries are sent to FormSubmit, a third-party service that processes the submission and forwards it to my email. FormSubmit may apply spam checks and retain information under its own privacy practices. Do not include sensitive personal or financial information. GitHub may keep standard server logs as the host.

What I do with your details

  • I use your name, email and inquiry only to reply and, if you go ahead, to deliver the project.
  • I don't sell your details or add you to a mailing list. Form submissions are shared with FormSubmit only for processing and delivery.
  • Files you send are kept only in a folder for your project and deleted 14 days after delivery, unless you ask me to keep them longer.
  • If we work through Fiverr or Upwork, that platform's own terms and privacy policy also apply.

Please don't send

Passwords, login details, SINs, bank or card numbers, or personal data about your clients or staff that the report doesn't need. Mask or delete those columns before sharing a file.

Questions or deletion requests

Email joeyolivari@gmail.com and I'll answer or delete your details.
