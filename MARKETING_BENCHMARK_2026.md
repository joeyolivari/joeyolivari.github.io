# Marketing benchmark and website motion brief (November 2026)

## Positioning
Primary audience: small businesses, contractors, nonprofit operators and managers who need dependable Excel cleanup, budget-vs-actual reporting, and updates they can maintain. Separate secondary audience: career clients seeking resume rewrites.

Proposed one-sentence positioning: Practical, traceable reports delivered by a Toronto-based analyst with over 10 years of financial reporting experience — with fixed-scope quotes and handover notes.

No third-party conversion uplift is assumed. Price references, case studies, and testimonials must be independently verified before claims are added.

## Design implementation
1. Home hero now includes an animated, conceptual *raw inputs → report-ready* illustration. Illustration is decorative, not a real client data claim.
2. Service and portfolio cards have subtle hover feedback, lift and image depth.
3. Pricing rows, process steps, forms and primary calls-to-action get small visual feedback cues.
4. Sections enter with one-shot lightweight animation when visible; without JS, content remains visible.
5. No infinite animation, autoplay video, floating overlay, or animation that displaces the quote button.
6. All effects honor reduced-motion preference. CSS uses opacity and transform for animated motion where possible.
